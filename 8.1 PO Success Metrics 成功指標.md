## Success Metrics

## 📊 Success Metrics Documentation Overview

### 1\. Introduction & Value Creation Overview (English)

- **Purpose:** Explains the core thinking behind success metrics for product teams.
- **Key Points:**
	- Success metrics validate if a project achieved its objective (focus on outcomes, not just activity).
		- Avoids common misuses (e.g., using MAU as a default metric).
		- Stresses the importance of distinguishing real value creation from value redistribution/cannibalization.
		- Emphasizes aligning metrics with the correct product pillar (Acquisition, Engagement, Retention, Monetization, Market Expansion, Brand/Product Quality).
		- Introduces “New Market Utilization” as a diagnostic metric for incremental value.
- **Link:** [Introduction & Value Creation Overview](https://onetw.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/NFPO/pages/2132377659 "https://onetw.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/NFPO/pages/2132377659")

### 1\. 成功指標導論與價值創造觀念說明 (Traditional Chinese)

- **目的:** 說明產品成功指標的核心思維與判斷方式。
- **重點摘要:**
	- 成功指標用於驗證專案是否真正達成目標（強調結果而非行為）。
		- 避免常見誤用（如濫用 MAU）。
		- 強調分辨「真正創造價值」與「價值轉移／蠶食」的重要性。
		- 成功指標需對齊正確的產品支柱（獲客、參與、留存、變現、市場擴展、品牌/產品品質）。
		- “New Market Utilization” 是用來診斷增量價值的指標。

### 2\. Success Metrics Guidelines (English)

- **Purpose:** Defines the standard approach for setting, tracking, and reviewing success metrics for all product initiatives.
- **Key Points:**
	- Every project must state its primary product pillar and objective.
		- Select 1–3 success metrics per project, directly reflecting the objective.
		- Define projected targets and baselines using historical or comparable data.
		- All metrics must be trackable; if not, tracking becomes a delivery requirement.
		- Six-month post-delivery tracking period for all projects.
		- Clear ownership and accountability for metric tracking and iteration.
		- Post-tracking review determines whether to continue, pivot, or stop investment.
- **Link:** [Success Metrics Guidelines](https://onetw.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/NFPO/pages/2113667106 "https://onetw.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/NFPO/pages/2113667106")

### 2\. 產品成功指標指引 (Traditional Chinese)

- **目的:** 定義所有產品專案設定、追蹤與檢討成功指標的標準流程。
- **重點摘要:**
	- 每個專案需明確定義主要產品支柱與目標。
		- 每案選定 1–3 個成功指標，直接反映目標達成狀況。
		- 需設定預期目標與基準線，並以歷史或可比資料為依據。
		- 所有指標必須可追蹤，否則需納入交付需求。
		- 專案交付後進行 6 個月追蹤，並有明確負責人。
		- 追蹤期後依成效決定是否持續、調整或終止投資。
- **連結:** [產品成功指標指引](https://onetw.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/NFPO/pages/2113667106 "https://onetw.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/NFPO/pages/2113667106")
